Reserve Court SF

Built with Python, runs daily on AWS

San Francisco's public tennis courts are gone within seconds of booking opening. So I wrote a bot that books them for me, faster than any person can click.

Fair play I only booked courts I was actually going to play on. No extras, never resold. Public courts should stay public.
What the bot does every morning
  1. 7:58 AM Logs in to the booking site
  2. 7:59 AM Grabs the text-message verification code by itself
  3. 8:00 AM Booking opens: tries every court at once to grab an open one
  4. 8:00 AM ✓ Court reserved in ~1.5 seconds
100+courts booked in 3 months
